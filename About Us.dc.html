Who we are

Keyframe is a teaching brand for filmmakers and directors working with generative tools. We are a small team operating out of India, run by working practitioners rather than course marketers. Vishwas leads the teaching.

The site you are on, keyframe24.ai, is where we publish our courses and take enrollments. Everything sold under the Keyframe name is made by us.

02

What we sell

Digital educational products, delivered online. Nothing physical ships.

  • Online video courses. Pre-recorded lessons streamed in our course player, with any workbooks or reference files that go with them.
  • Access, priced per course. Prices are shown on the enrollment page in the currency you are billed in, taxes indicated where they apply.
  • Delivery is immediate. Access lands in your email as soon as payment clears, usually within minutes.
03

What we teach

The resistance to execution has collapsed, but the bar for ideas has gone up. One beautiful disconnected image anyone can make. A story told across frames is the profession.

So we teach the director's craft: how to read a scene, build continuity, hold an audience across a cut. Tools change every quarter and we cover the current ones, but the judgment underneath is what we are actually selling. We do not promise income, jobs, or outcomes. That part is on you.
